case: P2.2 carried-variable linearity analysis
fortad revision: fa8224c (main)
machine: acluster.tugraz.at, AMD EPYC 7282, taskset -c 1
compiler: GNU Fortran 12.2.0 -O3 -std=f2018

scope:
  The comparison isolates the linearity decision after affine loop
  normalisation. Without the decision, the normalized RK4 recurrence would
  retain one real state value per iteration in state_tape. With the decision,
  the state adjoint is propagated through the constant coefficient and no
  per-iteration state is stored. Stage locals are not counted: affine
  normalisation has already removed their history, so this record measures the
  additional saving due to carried-variable linearity rather than claiming it
  for the affine pass as well.

record:
  At n=1000, the counterfactual state tape is 1000 real values, or 8000 bytes.
  The emitted rk4_vjp source is 3012 bytes and contains no tape declaration;
  bytes saved by linearity are therefore 8000. The committed numeric record is
  results/p22_linearity_fortad.csv.

independent correctness oracle:
  The remote emitted VJP was compiled with gfortran and checked against a
  separately implemented RK4 primal by a central directional finite
  difference. At n=1000 the relative error was
  5.1290690228009139E-11 (threshold 1.0E-8).

decision:
  Keep the strict linearity test: a carried variable is tape-free only when
  every derivative coefficient is built from inactive values. A recurrence
  such as u = u*exp(a(i)) remains taped because its derivative with respect to
  active a(i) reads the old u, even though the primal expression is formally
  linear in u. The RK4 result is the positive case and the nonlinear
  recurrence in p21_tbr_fortad.csv is the negative case.
