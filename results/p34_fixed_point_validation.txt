scope:
  The structured call-rule registry is used for the two-state tanh map
  x=G(x,p). The tangent callback iterates the linearized map to solve
  (I-G_x)dx=G_p dp. The adjoint callback is the second Christianson phase: it
  iterates lambda=G_x^T lambda+u and returns G_p^T lambda. The opaque primal
  fixed-point iteration is not taped by the generated derivative.

decision and boundary:
  Keep the two-phase rule explicit and caller-supplied. Require a convergent
  fixed-point map and leave map derivatives, stopping criteria, and any
  acceleration to the registrant. Automatic map extraction and general
  shaped-array support remain future work.
