  independently validates the complete eight-input/24-residual consumer by

decision:
  Close P5.3 at the optimiser integration boundary. Do not add a generic
  optimiser driver to fortad or fortnum: the application owns the iteration
  policy and data layout, while the callback contract is stable and tested.
