scope:
  The frozen surface is the source-transforming fortad module and CLI at
  version 0.1.0. The documented mapping keeps ADOL-C's jacobian, hessian,
  jac_vec, vec_jac, hess_vec, and sparse names as product terminology while
  retaining fortad's source-returning fad_* calls. Literal runtime-tape aliases
  and a generic driver are intentionally not exposed.

decision:

  versioned roadmap change; do not add ADOL-C runtime compatibility aliases,
